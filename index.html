The commercial problem

The difficult part is not seeing that you are short. It is understanding why.

A supplier may invoice R500,000 and receive R445,000. The R55,000 difference may be legitimate - or some of it may not be. Finance still has to determine what happened.

R500,000Supplier invoice
R445,000Payment received
R55,000Difference to explain

Fragmented evidence

Claims, remittances, invoices, PODs, GRVs, trading terms, promotions and credit notes may all hold part of the answer.

Manual investigation

Experienced finance staff spend time assembling information before they can make a commercial decision.

Limited visibility

Management may lack one clear view of outstanding, disputed, accepted and actually recovered value.

How VEYRIN works

From fragmented data to focused action

The objective is not to remove commercial judgement. It is to reduce repetitive investigation and present the right evidence when a person needs to decide.
