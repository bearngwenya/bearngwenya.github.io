The commercial problem

A short payment is easy to see. Understanding it is the hard part.

A supplier may invoice R500,000 and receive R445,000. The R55,000 difference may be completely legitimate - or some of it may not be. Finance still has to determine what happened.

R500,000Supplier invoice
R445,000Payment received
R55,000Difference to explain
Repetitive investigation
Experienced finance staff can spend hours finding and matching evidence.
Ageing deductions
Cases can sit unresolved while more urgent work takes priority.
Missed exceptions
Possible duplicates, mismatches or unsupported deductions can be overlooked.
Limited management visibility
Outstanding, disputed and recovered value can remain spread across files and systems.

The problem is not that finance teams are not doing the work. The problem is that too much of the work still has to be assembled manually.
