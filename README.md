# VEYRIN

Financial Intelligence Platform

VEYRIN is a research-stage financial intelligence platform designed to help organisations understand financially material differences when evidence is fragmented across systems, files, documents and teams.

VEYRIN is being designed as an independent intelligence layer that can work alongside existing accounting, ERP, banking, spreadsheet, document and operational environments. It is ERP-compatible, not ERP-dependent.

The initial research and proof area focuses on commercial settlement and financially material exceptions. This is a validation starting point, not the limit of the broader platform vision.

Human teams retain authority over consequential financial decisions. Named integrations and production capabilities are only represented once verified and available.

Public research-stage website. Strategic architecture, commercial models and internal research are intentionally not published here.
